Ujjwal Pawar

PhD researcher building and operating large-scale mobile network systems

I am a PhD student at the University of Edinburgh, working in the NetSys Lab under the supervision of Prof. Mahesh K. Marina.

My research focuses on Open RAN and private 5G systems, with an emphasis on using real-world deployments to study mobile network operation, measurement, and efficiency. I contributed to Campus5G, a campus-scale O-RAN-compliant private 5G testbed deployed across the University of Edinburgh’s central campus.

I work across RAN optimization, telemetry, cloud-based emulation, and AI-driven network automation. I am currently an AI/ML Research Intern in Open RAN and 6G Communications at Keysight Technologies.

Outside research, I love building mechanical keyboards and will probably ask what kind of key switches your keyboard has.
